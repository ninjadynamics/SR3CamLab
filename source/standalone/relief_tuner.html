One head per tone family. Drag to turn or tilt the surface, scroll over it to zoom, tune its depth, and compare ON / OFF. Facades, walls and asphalt start at 1.0×; everything else starts at 4.0×. Your choices apply to all listed tone variants when exported.

Existing slider choices are converted to approximately preserve their previous relief strength; use Default to try the new baseline. Notes and camera positions are retained. Facades share one calibration to preserve their authored depth hierarchy. Normalization balances strength; it does not repair incorrectly inferred features.
