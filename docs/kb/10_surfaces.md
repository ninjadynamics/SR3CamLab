# 10 - Surface types: which texture id selects which physics surface

Generated by `work/scripts/gen_surface_doc.py` from `surfaces.py` output. Source of truth: the two lists the exe
walks in 0x5B1820 - `SurfacePairEffects\surfacepairdata_xdata.sbf`, root +0x2C (linked list `{next, name hash, global
index, name}`) and root +0x30 (parallel linked list `{next, count, texture ids}`), 90 entries. VERIFIED: every one of the
road-layer ids of the 6 arcade tracks, the 2 Revo demo tracks and the 24 converted Revo tracks is found in these lists
except 2 (Canyon4 008a2850, one in Lakeside4), which fall back to global surface 0.

How a road cell uses them (04_trackdeform_road.md): byte +0x18 of a cell is its BASE layer (what is under the
surface, shown where the road is worn/deformed), byte +0x19 its TOP layer. VERIFIED by names: in every track the
+0x18 ids resolve to `..._Base` entries and the +0x19 ids to `..._Top` entries (a few Desert4/Lakeside cells use a
Top entry in both). An importer picks a (base id, top id) pair that SEGA uses together, lists both ids in the road
chunk tables, and must ship both texture chunks (kind 4, DXT5 1024x1024) under those ids; the pixels may be replaced.

Track name prefixes below: none = arcade track, `D:` = Revo PC demo, `R:` = Revo PS3 track converted to PC
(`F:\Jogos\_revo_ps3_converted`). Cell counts are road cells using the id in that track.

## Boundary (wall) surfaces

The wall chunk has its own id table (06_boundary_collision.md). Those ids are looked up in the same file, root +0x04:
a linked list of 31 groups `{next, ->{count, ids}, flag, 0}`, parallel to the 31 names of root +0x14
(`CarToCar_*`, `Boundary_Armco, Brick, Metal, Mud, Plastic, Rock, Tyres, Wood, Vegetation, Snow, None`, then
per-environment variants). Group membership found: floor/ceiling ids of Stadium (ee64dc3d), Alpine/Lakeside/Desert
(a76a4869), Tropical (2d1caef9), Canyon (7d91807d) are all in group 0; Stadium walls e2671eda in group 20, 3ac3a983 in
group 6; Lakeside walls 5648af3a in group 5. The group -> name pairing was not pinned down (UNKNOWN); the builder
simply reuses the slot's own most common wall id and floor id.
